To: Anand Component Works Pvt. Ltd. (General Counsel)

Re: Termination of Manufacturing Supply Agreement with Ashoka Forge Works Pvt. Ltd. - Cure Period Strategy
Date: 6 May 2022

Anand Component Works Pvt. Ltd. ("Anand"), a company with its registered office in Pune, Maharashtra, seeks advice on terminating its five-year manufacturing supply agreement with Ashoka Forge Works Pvt. Ltd. ("Ashoka") for repeated quality failures.
## PAGE 2
1. BACKGROUND

The supply agreement with Ashoka contains a termination-for-convenience clause with a forty (40) day cure period, materially similar in structure to cure-period clauses Anand uses in its own supplier agreements. Anand issued a notice of termination for convenience on 1 March 2022, citing repeated quality failures as its commercial reason, and the forty-day cure period expired on 10 April 2022 without the quality concerns being addressed.

2. ANALYSIS

Ashoka has not yet asserted any damages claim, pre-emptive or otherwise. We advise Anand may proceed to terminate under the cure-period mechanism once formally elapsed, consistent with the reasoning in Continental Constructions Co. Ltd v. State Trading Corporation of India, AIR 1997 Del 217, subject to the caveats discussed in our standing advice on that authority's more recent treatment.
