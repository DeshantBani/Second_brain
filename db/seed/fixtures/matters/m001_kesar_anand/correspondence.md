## PAGE 1
[Letterhead: Anand Component Works Pvt. Ltd. - Legal Department]

18 November 2021

Kesar Industries Pvt. Ltd.
By Email and Registered Post

Re: Notice of Breach dated 15 September 2021 - Manufacturing Supply Agreement dated 4 April 2019

Dear Sirs,

We write on behalf of Anand Component Works Pvt. Ltd. in response to your notice of breach dated 15 September 2021 and the expiry of the forty-five (45) day cure period referenced therein on 30 October 2021.

Our client's position is that the shortfall in offtake was directly caused by Kesar's own pattern of quality rejections during the relevant period, which rendered a substantial proportion of tendered units unusable. In these circumstances, our client reserves the right to claim damages of approximately INR 3.2 crore in the event Kesar proceeds to terminate the Agreement.
