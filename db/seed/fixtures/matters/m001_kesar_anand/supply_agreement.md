MANUFACTURING SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Manufacturing Supply Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 4 April 2019 between Kesar Industries Pvt. Ltd. ("Supplier"), a company incorporated under the laws of India with its registered office in Pune, Maharashtra, and Anand Component Works Pvt. Ltd. ("Buyer"), a company incorporated under the laws of India with its registered office in Pune, Maharashtra.

WHEREAS the Supplier manufactures precision-machined automotive components and the Buyer wishes to procure such components on a continuing basis for a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date, the parties agree as follows.

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, and the courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

ARTICLE 3 - TERM

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 14, shall continue for a period of five (5) years.

ARTICLE 8 - PRICING AND VOLUME COMMITMENTS

8.1 The Buyer commits to a minimum annual offtake of 240,000 units across the component families listed in Schedule A, at the pricing set out in Schedule B, subject to an annual escalation not exceeding 6%.

ARTICLE 14 - TERMINATION

14.1 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not capable of remedy, or commits a bankruptcy or insolvency event.

14.2 Termination for Convenience with Cure Period. Either party may terminate this Agreement for its own convenience and without needing to establish cause, by giving the other party written notice of its intention to exit the relationship. Where the terminating party's notice identifies a specific performance concern as the commercial reason for the exit (including, without limitation, a shortfall against the minimum offtake commitment under Article 8, or a quality concern under Schedule C), the other party shall have a period of forty-five (45) days from the date of such notice (the "Cure Period") to address the identified concern. If the concern is not addressed within the Cure Period, the terminating party may proceed to terminate this Agreement by further written notice, without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available to it, including a claim for damages.

14.3 No claim for damages related to a concern identified in a notice under Clause 14.2 shall be asserted by either party until the Cure Period has expired without that concern having been addressed - the purpose of the Cure Period would otherwise be defeated.

ARTICLE 17 - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

17.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, save in cases of wilful default or gross negligence.

ARTICLE 22 - DISPUTE RESOLUTION

22.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that is not resolved by good-faith negotiation within thirty (30) days shall be referred to arbitration in Pune under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
