MEMORANDUM

To: Meridian Pharma Ltd. (Procurement Director)
From: Advisory Team
Re: Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient Supply Agreement - Annual Price Renegotiation
Date: 14 March 2023

Meridian Pharma Ltd. ("Meridian"), based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, holds a five-year Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient supply agreement with a Chinese API manufacturer, and seeks advice on invoking the agreement's annual price-renegotiation mechanism in light of a sharp rise in input costs.
## PAGE 2
1. BACKGROUND

The supply agreement is in its second year of a five-year term and remains in good standing - there has been no breach by either party, no notice of termination has been given or contemplated by either side, and both parties intend the relationship to continue. Clause 11 of the agreement provides for an annual price review triggered by either party, based on a benchmark input-cost index.

2. ANALYSIS

This is a routine commercial price-adjustment exercise under a performing contract, not a termination or breach scenario - no cure period, termination notice, or damages claim is in issue at all. We recommend Meridian invoke the Clause 11 review mechanism formally in writing, supported by index data, and does not require any assessment of termination rights at this stage.
