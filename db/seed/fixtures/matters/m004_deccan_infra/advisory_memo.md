Re: EPC Contract - Final Milestone Payment Dispute - Arbitration Strategy
Date: 9 September 2022

Deccan Infra Projects Ltd. ("Deccan"), an EPC contractor based in Hyderabad, Telangana, is in active arbitration against Ganga Power Corporation over withheld final-milestone payments of approximately INR 18 crore under an Engineering, Procurement and Construction contract for a 50 MW solar installation.
## PAGE 2
1. BACKGROUND

Ganga Power has withheld the final milestone payment, alleging that Deccan's work did not meet the performance-ratio guarantee in Schedule D of the EPC contract. Deccan disputes this, relying on third-party inspection reports. Arbitration proceedings commenced in April 2022 under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and are ongoing before a three-member tribunal seated in Hyderabad.

2. ANALYSIS

This is a performance-guarantee dispute under an EPC contract, not a termination dispute - there is no termination notice in issue and no cure-period mechanism engaged, since neither party has sought to terminate the underlying contract. The relevant question is evidentiary: whether the performance-ratio guarantee was met, based on competing technical inspection reports. We recommend Deccan focus its arbitration strategy on establishing the independence and methodology of its third-party inspector ahead of the merits hearing.

3. AWARD-CHALLENGE EXPOSURE

Should the tribunal rule against Deccan and Deccan later consider a Section 34 challenge, we note the public-policy ground for setting aside an award as articulated in ONGC v. Saw Pipes Ltd, (2003) 5 SCC 705, which permits interference only where the award is patently illegal or contrary to the fundamental policy of Indian law - a high bar that a mere disagreement with the tribunal's assessment of the inspection evidence is unlikely to meet.
