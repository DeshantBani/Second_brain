To: Bansal Foods Pvt. Ltd. (Managing Director)

Re: Termination of Regional Distribution Agreement with Shetty Distributors - Termination for Cause
Date: 22 June 2020

Bansal Foods Pvt. Ltd. ("Bansal"), a company with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka, entered into a three-year Regional Distribution Agreement with Shetty Distributors on 1 July 2018 for the distribution of packaged snack products across Karnataka.
## PAGE 2
1. BACKGROUND

Bansal has discovered that Shetty Distributors has been reselling Bansal's products outside the agreed territory in breach of Clause 9 (Territorial Exclusivity) of the Distribution Agreement, and has also failed to remit collections on time on three occasions. The Distribution Agreement contains a termination-for-cause clause (Clause 16) permitting immediate termination on written notice for a breach of the territorial exclusivity obligation, with no cure period applicable to that specific breach - cure periods under this agreement apply only to payment-related breaches, and even then only for fifteen (15) days.

2. ANALYSIS

Because the breach here (territorial exclusivity) falls within the immediate-termination category under Clause 16 and is not subject to a cure period at all, Bansal may terminate with immediate effect on written notice specifying the breach. This is a materially different termination mechanism from a termination-for-convenience-with-cure-period structure: there is no cure window during which Shetty could remedy the exclusivity breach, and no scope for a race between a damages claim and an expiring cure period. We recommend immediate written notice of termination under Clause 16, with a reservation of rights to pursue damages for the exclusivity breach separately.
