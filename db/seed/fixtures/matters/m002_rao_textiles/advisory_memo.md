MEMORANDUM

To: Rao Textiles Pvt. Ltd. (HR Director)
From: Advisory Team
Re: Termination of Senior Plant Manager - Wrongful Termination Exposure
Date: 3 February 2022

Rao Textiles Pvt. Ltd. ("Rao"), a company with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka, seeks advice on terminating the employment of its Senior Plant Manager, Mr. K. Subramaniam, for repeated failure to meet production targets at its Peenya facility.
## PAGE 2
1. BACKGROUND

Mr. Subramaniam has been employed by Rao since 2015 under a written employment contract governed by the laws of Karnataka. His most recent performance review flagged a sustained shortfall against production targets over three consecutive quarters. Rao's employment contract with Mr. Subramaniam contains a notice period of ninety (90) days for termination without cause, and permits summary termination only for proven misconduct.

2. ANALYSIS

Rao has not alleged misconduct, only underperformance, and there is no cure-period mechanism in this employment contract of the kind found in commercial supply agreements. Termination without cause requires full service of the ninety-day notice period or payment in lieu. We see no basis on the facts presented for summary termination, and recommend proceeding via the notice-period route, supported by a documented performance-improvement plan to reduce wrongful-termination exposure under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 to the extent applicable.
