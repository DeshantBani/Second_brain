Our client is Kesar Industries, which has a five-year manufacturing supply agreement
with Anand Component Works, governed by Indian law. The agreement contains a
termination for convenience clause with a thirty (30) day cure period.

Anand Component Works has now threatened to raise a damages claim against Kesar
Industries pre-emptively - that is, before the thirty-day cure period under the
agreement has even run its full course.

Kesar Industries wants to file a civil suit for permanent injunction and declaration
before the District Court at Pune, restraining Anand Component Works from
terminating the agreement or pursuing any damages claim until the full cure period
has run, and seeking costs of the suit. Kesar Industries is the Plaintiff and Anand
Component Works is the Defendant.
